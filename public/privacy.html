Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This page explains what information we collect when you use our AI property assistant and browse listings, and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Information you type into the chat (your questions about properties)
  • If you request contact from a consultant: your name and phone number
  • If you request a viewing: your preferred date and time
  • Which properties you view, search for, or mark as a favorite (favorites are stored only in your own browser, not on our servers)

How we use it

We use this information solely to help you find a property and to let one of our consultants contact you if you've asked for that. We do not sell your information to third parties or use it for advertising.

Where it's stored

Your information is stored securely in our database (Supabase, an EU/US-based infrastructure provider) and is only accessible to our team.

Your choices

You can browse and use the AI assistant without sharing any personal details — contact information is only collected if you choose to request a viewing or ask us to reach out. If you'd like your information removed, contact us using the details on our site.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out through the contact options on our main site.
